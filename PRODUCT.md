# Product

## Register

product

## Users

The agency's own team: strategists, designers, copywriters and account leads. Clients never log in; BrandOS is internal only. They use it at a laptop or a large monitor during the working day, usually mid-task: someone on a call needs the right landing page, a designer needs this brand's colours and logo, an account lead wants to know what is stuck before a client meeting.

The job on every screen is orientation and retrieval, not management:
- Where am I, what belongs here, what can I do next?
- What exists for this client, brand, service or offer, and where is it used?
- What is waiting on me, and what has been stuck in review?
- Get the right asset (download, open, link, reuse) in as few clicks as possible.

Time, tasks and deadlines live in ClickUp and Asana. BrandOS owns meaning: positioning, what supports it, and whether it is ready.

## Product Purpose

One place where every client, brand, service, offer and asset the agency works on is organised the way the team thinks about it: Client → Brand (→ sub-brand) → Service → Offer ↔ Asset, with each brand's kit, a shared Global Library, a review flow, and a funnel board per offer.

Success looks like: a new team member can say within five seconds where they are, what belongs there and what to do next; nobody rebuilds an asset that already exists; nothing sits in review unnoticed; and the team reaches for BrandOS before Drive or Slack search.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, crafted.

Quiet confidence in the manner of Apple and Linear: nothing shouts, everything is exactly placed, and the details feel considered. Copy is plain, specific and calm; it says what happens ("Linking does not copy anything") and never sells. No exclamation marks, no "Oops", no marketing voice inside the product.

Each brand is a "different building": inside a brand the interface takes on that brand's colours, and leaving it returns to the neutral agency colour. That shift is the one moment of expressiveness; everything else stays restrained.

References to be measured against: apple.com (restraint, typography, spacing), Ramp (clean, fast product UI), Linear (density, speed, keyboard-first, precise detail).

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS templates: hero metrics, stat tiles that repeat numbers shown elsewhere, "0 of 0" tiles, identical icon-and-heading card grids, gradient cards, stock spot illustrations, emoji.
- Project management: due-date drivers, overdue states, assignees beyond the reviewer, kanban boards, sprints, time tracking, task calendars.
- Dark "AI tool" shells, neon accents, glassmorphism, decorative gradients.
- Dense enterprise DAM screens: facet panels with dozens of checkboxes, metadata-schema editors, usage analytics.
- Anything client-facing ("Share with client", public links): clients never log in.
- Red for "incomplete". Red means broken or destructive only.
- Repetition: the same navigation, header, count or action shown twice on one page.

## Design Principles

1. **Lead with what the person came for.** The list, the queue, the asset; never stats first. One question per screen, and anything that doesn't answer it goes.
2. **Show what is stuck, not what is due.** "In review for 12 days" beats a deadline. BrandOS owns meaning; ClickUp owns time.
3. **One thing, one place.** An asset exists once and is linked, never copied; a number is shown once; a rule lives in one module. Absence is made visible (gaps, "No entry point") instead of hidden.
4. **Context is pre-filled.** Wherever the person already is (a brand, an offer, a folder) the next action already knows it, and says so.
5. **Calm by default, expressive by brand.** Neutral agency surfaces; each brand's colour only inside that brand. One primary action per area; destructive actions out of the way and admin-only; structure is archived, never deleted.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG 2.2 AA: body text at least 4.5:1, including muted text on tinted surfaces and on light brand colours (accents are darkened per brand until they read).
- Visible focus on every interactive element; full keyboard use (⌘K and "/" to search, Esc closes any panel, keyboard alternatives for every drag).
- Respect reduced motion: motion is decoration only and snaps when reduced.
- Works from 390px phones to large monitors without sideways scrolling.
- Status never relies on colour alone: dots always come with a word.
